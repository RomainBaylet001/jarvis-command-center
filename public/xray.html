METHODOLOGY / TRANSPARENCY

Every percentage has a source.

01

Your portfolio

Your locally saved VWCE and VFEA purchases determine each ETF's weight in your portfolio. Available EUR closing prices are used for current valuation.

02

Inside each ETF

Official Vanguard country allocations are applied to each ETF. These are dated issuer disclosures, not intraday geographic updates.

03

Combined exposure

Each country's portfolio exposure equals its allocation within each ETF, multiplied by that ETF's portfolio weight. SXR8 is excluded because it is your benchmark.
