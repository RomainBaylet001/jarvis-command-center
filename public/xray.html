How it’s calculated

Method and sources

Your ETF weights

VWCE and VFEA are weighted by their available market values. If a complete valuation is unavailable, invested capital is used and labelled. SXR8 is excluded.

Country exposure

Each ETF’s portfolio weight is multiplied by its disclosed country allocation. The results are added across your ETFs.

Data limits

Issuer allocations are dated snapshots. Undisclosed countries remain in “Other markets”; missing weights do not mean zero exposure. Country classifications do not describe every company’s sources of revenue.
